Founding Partner

20 years of hands-on experience in hardware and consumer-app investing and fundraising for global markets, through complete business cycles.

From product definition, R&D and manufacturing, to crowdfunding, user growth and team building; from logistics, warehousing and cross-border platform operations, to branding, affiliate marketing, online distribution and offline wholesale — we have lived every core step of global expansion, and know what fundraising and investing really mean for founders.

We have also embedded that industry experience into a continuously running AI agent system. One agent scans early-stage signals around the world; one tracks the shifting preferences of Chinese investors; one curates funding trends and key developments in the AI going-global space; and one makes sure no project that enters our radar is ever dropped. Traditional FAs rely on networks and gut feel. On top of that, we run a tireless information network — making matches more precise and fundraising timelines more controllable.

We believe that only people who truly understand the industry, understand going global, and understand entrepreneurship — while using technology to move faster — can be the most trustworthy partner and comrade-in-arms for a new generation of founders.
